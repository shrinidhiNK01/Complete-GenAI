GenAI End to End Pipeline:-
==============================
1)Data Acquisition :- Available data(csv,txt,pdf,docs,xlsx) or Other data(DB,internet,API,scrapping) or No data(Create ur own data)Perform Data Augmentation(difference variance of a same data) or Biagram flip(Simply increasing the data with meaning)    or Back translation(converting the text to another language, and again back to the original language data) and additnal data /additional Noise

2)Data Preparation:- 1)data cleaning(html,emoji,spelling correction),
                     2)Basic preprocessing :- 1)Tokenisation(Sentance,word)
                                              2)Optional Preprcessing:-
                                                 1)stop word removal
                                                 2)steaming
                                                 3)lamatization
                                                 4)punctuation removal
                                                 5)lower case
                                                 6)language detection
                     3)advance preprocessing:- 1)Parts of speech training
                                               2)Parsing
                                               3)Coreference resolution
3)Feature Engineering:- Text vectorization:-1)TFIDF
                                            2)Bug of word
                                            3)One hot
                                            4)Transformers Model
                                            5)wordzvee
4)Modeling :- Use Opensource LLM or Paid LLM
5)Evaluation :- Intrinsive or Extrinsive(Deploy)
6)Deployment
7)Monitoring and Model Updating

Image Augumentation is a step where Augmentation are applied to existing images in our datasets this process can help improve the ability of ur model to generalize and thus perfoem more effective on unseen images
